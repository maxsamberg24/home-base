Privacy Policy

The Locker Room · https://the-lockerroom.netlify.app · Last updated October 7, 2026

The Locker Room is a personal sports app for following your favorite teams and playing games with friends. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Display name you choose when you start using the app.
  • Teams you follow, your picks, predictions, notes, group memberships, and friend connections.
  • Phone number — only if you choose to turn on text alerts. We also store your alert preferences and a record of which alerts were sent so you never get duplicates.
  • Photos or other content you voluntarily add to the app.

We do not collect payment information, and we do not require an email address or password.

How we use it

  • To run the app: showing your teams, schedules, standings, friends, and games.
  • To send the text alerts you signed up for (for example game starting, score updates, and final scores) for the teams and alert types you choose, and to send a one-time verification code to confirm your number.

Text messages (SMS)

  • You receive texts only after you enter your phone number on the Notify page, confirm it with a code we text you, and select the alerts you want.
  • Message frequency varies with the teams and alerts you choose (typically a few messages on game days). Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts. Reply HELP for help. You can also remove your number or turn off alerts on the Notify page.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or text-messaging opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is never shared with third parties for marketing.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We use service providers only to operate the app: Twilio (to deliver text messages), Netlify (hosting and database), and ESPN and other public sports data sources (we send them team and game requests, not your personal information). Other users in your friend groups can see your display name, the teams you follow, and the picks and content you share with them.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information while you use the app. To have your phone number or account information deleted, email us at the address below and we will remove it.

Children

The Locker Room is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes
